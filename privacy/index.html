Privacy policy

Effective September 3, 2026

This policy covers SleepLab, a private test project for one person, and its informational website. SleepLab is operated by its account owner for their own analysis. It is in development and does not offer accounts or services to other users.

Information used

With the owner's authorization, SleepLab may retrieve Oura sleep records, activity and readiness summaries, heart-rate records, workout records and user-entered tags or notes. It uses only the access granted through Oura's consent process. The local workspace may also contain other sleep and health records supplied by the owner, including personal notes.

OAuth credentials are used to authorize the connection and are stored in the owner's macOS Keychain. They are not published on this website or included in the public site files.

Purpose

Records are used for personal sleep analysis, exploring potential improvements to sleep, and developing and testing the owner's private workspace. SleepLab does not sell records, use them for advertising, or use them to train or fine-tune AI models.

Storage and protection

Original health-data files and the analysis database are stored in the owner's local workspace. Health records and credentials are excluded from the published site and from source-control commits. Access to local records depends on the owner's computer access controls and backup settings.

Sharing and optional AI assistance

The informational website does not receive health records. Oura processes requests made to its service under its own policies. The owner may choose to share records with a clinician or another service.

If the owner directs Codex or another cloud assistant to inspect local files, selected file contents or prompts may be sent to that provider. That is separate from publishing this website and is governed by the owner's instructions, the provider's terms and settings, and the applicable Oura authorization. This policy does not represent such AI-assisted analysis as entirely offline.

Retention, revocation and deletion

Local records are retained for the owner's analysis until the owner deletes them or an applicable authorization requires their removal. The owner can revoke SleepLab's access through Oura. After revocation, collection and use of the affected Oura records must cease unless access is authorized again.

To delete local Oura data, stop the connection and remove the original Oura exports, related database records, derived outputs and any copies in backups. The owner controls these files directly. Data requested for deletion must not be retained. Removing local copies does not delete records held in the owner's Oura account.

Visits to this website

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages. It contains no forms, advertising, embedded analytics or third-party scripts. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes; its handling of hosting-related information is described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Contact and changes

The account owner operates this personal installation and controls its records. The contact email registered for the SleepLab Oura application is the contact for privacy and support questions. The project's GitHub maintainer is mikeuduc.

This policy will be updated if the project's data handling changes. A broader service or additional data use would require updated disclosures and appropriate authorization.
